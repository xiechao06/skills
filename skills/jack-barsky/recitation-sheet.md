# 📚 Vocabulary Recitation Sheet

## consist
1. "A healthy breakfast **consists of** protein, fiber, and fruit."
2. "Success doesn't **consist in** how much money you have, but in how happy you are."
3. "Life **consists of** small moments, not big ones."
4. "What does true happiness **consist in**?"

## nuance
1. "The difference between 'anger' and 'frustration' is a matter of **nuance**."
2. "He's a good writer, but he lacks **nuance** — everything is black and white."
3. "You need to read between the lines to catch the **nuances** of what she's saying."
4. "There's a **nuance** to sarcasm that's hard to grasp in a second language."

## assiduously
1. "She **assiduously** avoided junk food for three months before the wedding."
2. "The gardener **assiduously** trimmed every hedge to perfection."
3. "He **assiduously** practiced piano until his fingers ached."
4. "I've been **assiduously** saving money to buy a house."

## perseverance
1. "Rome wasn't built in a day — it takes **perseverance**."
2. "She achieved her dream through sheer **perseverance**."
3. "Learning English requires patience and **perseverance**."
4. "His **perseverance** in the face of failure is truly inspiring."

## division
1. "The company is split into three main **divisions**: sales, marketing, and engineering."
2. "There's a growing **division** between the rich and the poor."
3. "The **division** of labor made the project much more efficient."
4. "The river forms a natural **division** between the two towns."

## enact
1. "The children love to **enact** their favorite fairy tales."
2. "The government plans to **enact** a new law on data privacy."
3. "History repeats itself — the same drama gets **enacted** over and over."
4. "It's easier to **enact** a policy than to enforce it."

## clutter
1. "My mind is full of **clutter** — I can't think straight."
2. "Don't **clutter** your room with things you never use."
3. "A clean desk equals a clear mind — get rid of the **clutter**."
4. "Too many notifications **clutter** my phone screen."

## jumble
1. "My thoughts were a **jumble** of excitement and fear."
2. "The letters of the word are all **jumbled** up — can you unscramble them?"
3. "There's a **jumble** of cables behind my TV."
4. "After the party, the living room was a complete **jumble**."

## incomprehensible
1. "Why would anyone do that? It's utterly **incomprehensible** to me."
2. "The instruction manual was written in **incomprehensible** technical jargon."
3. "His decision to quit his job without a backup plan is **incomprehensible**."
4. "She spoke so fast that her words were **incomprehensible**."

## facade
1. "Behind his brave **facade**, he was actually terrified."
2. "Their marriage was just a **facade** — they hadn't been happy in years."
3. "Social media is often a **facade**; people only show the best parts of their lives."
4. "Don't judge a building by its **facade**."

## tautological
1. "'Free gift' is **tautological** — a gift is already free by definition."
2. "Saying 'unexpected surprise' is **tautological** because surprises are always unexpected."
3. "Politicians love **tautological** phrases like 'it is what it is'."
4. "Avoid **tautological** expressions in your writing — they make you sound repetitive."

## twofold
1. "The benefits of exercise are **twofold**: it improves your body and your mind."
2. "Our profits have increased **twofold** since last year."
3. "The reason I'm learning English is **twofold** — for work and for travel."
4. "The population of the city has grown **twofold** in the past decade."
